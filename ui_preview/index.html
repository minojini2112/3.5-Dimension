How it works

Three steps from pencil to 3D

1. Draw or upload

Rough lines are fine. Add a few words if you want a colour or material.

2. AI fills in the object

Your outline becomes a full picture with colour, light and material.

3. Get your 3D model

The hidden sides are worked out for you. Spin it, then download it.
